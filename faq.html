FAQ

Clear answers before you submit.

Repost Promotion is selective, simple, and guided by public standards.

What is Repost Promotion?

Repost Promotion is a community repost platform dedicated to helping quality content reach a wider audience through reviewed, responsible reposts.

Does every post get reposted?

No. Reposts are selective and depend on quality, relevance, safety, originality, and alignment with the community guidelines.

Is the service free?

The public submission process is free unless an official project update announces a different option in the future.

How long does review take?

Review timing can vary based on volume and availability. A lack of response does not mean a creator is blocked from future consideration.

Can businesses participate?

Yes. Businesses can submit professional, truthful, relevant posts that follow the same standards as creator and community submissions.

Can I submit multiple posts?

Yes, but submissions should be thoughtful. Repeated low-quality tagging, duplicates, or aggressive promotion may reduce consideration.

How are reposts selected?

Selection considers usefulness, clarity, authenticity, audience fit, safety, and whether reposting would improve the community feed.

Can decisions be appealed?

Formal appeals may be added in the future. For now, creators can improve alignment with the guidelines and submit future posts for review.
