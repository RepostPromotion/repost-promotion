Copyright

Respect for creators is a core standard.

Repost Promotion expects submitters to own, license, or have permission to share the work they request for amplification.

Respect for Creators

Creators deserve clear attribution and control over their work. Repost Promotion may decline or remove content that appears to misuse another person's intellectual property.

DMCA

Copyright owners may submit notices through the relevant third-party platform where the content appears. If a dedicated project contact method is later published, copyright instructions should be updated here.

Copyright Complaints

A useful complaint should identify the copyrighted work, the allegedly infringing post, the rights owner or authorized representative, and enough detail to evaluate the request.

Removal Requests

When a credible copyright or creator-rights concern is identified, Repost Promotion may remove or avoid reposting the relevant content.
