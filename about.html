About

Built to make quality easier to discover.

Repost Promotion exists to help strong posts reach people who can learn from them, use them, join them, or support them.

Mission

Our mission is to amplify content that is useful, original, clear, and responsibly presented. Reposting should create value for both the creator and the audience.

Vision

We believe online discovery can be more intentional. A trusted repost channel can help thoughtful creators grow without rewarding spam, impersonation, or low-effort promotion.

Purpose

The platform gives creators a simple way to request consideration while giving the community a clear set of standards for what may be shared.

Community Focus

Repost Promotion is designed around people, not engagement tricks. The project favors posts that add context, clarity, creativity, or practical value.

Transparency

Selection is never guaranteed. Public guidelines explain the review principles, and future updates will continue to make the process easier to understand.

Quality Standards

Strong submissions respect creators, avoid misleading claims, and present links, offers, or calls to action honestly.
