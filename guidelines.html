Guidelines

Responsible promotion starts with clear standards.

These guidelines protect creators, audiences, and the reputation of the community.

Acceptable Content

Submissions should be original or properly attributed, relevant to a real audience, professionally presented, and safe for a broad community. Educational posts, creator work, product updates, launches, thoughtful commentary, resources, and community announcements are welcome when they add value.

Prohibited Content

Repost Promotion does not accept spam, scams, illegal content, impersonation, malicious links, malware, hateful content, harassment, copyright violations, deceptive offers, or content designed to manipulate engagement.

No Spam

Repeated tagging, duplicate posts, irrelevant replies, or pressure tactics may reduce future consideration.

No Scams or Unsafe Links

Posts that mislead users, hide material terms, direct people to harmful destinations, or promote malware are not eligible.

No Harassment or Hate

Content that targets people or groups with abuse, threats, dehumanizing language, or discriminatory attacks is not compatible with the community.

No Copyright Violations

Creators must have the right to share the work they submit. Respectful attribution and prompt removal requests matter.
